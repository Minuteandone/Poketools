The original development resources include Maya-exported Nintendo DS .imd model data. This viewer constructs real meshes from their vertex and primitive instructions, including embedded palette textures where supported. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom.

These are selected *actual* 3D resource files, not images pretending to be models. Asset selection does not download an entire NARC.
