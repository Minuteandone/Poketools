How this works

NCGR/NCBR graphics, NCLR palettes, NCER cells and the original animation/map data are combined in the browser. Changing a version downloads its corresponding archived NARC, not a flattened screenshot.

Why some poses may look unusual

Early development graphics can be incomplete or misassembled. This tool shows the underlying recorded resources; no claim is made that every prototype was used in a playable build.
